Privacy policy and terms

Fitbit Air HR is a personal app used only by its owner.

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's Google Health workout and heart-rate data.
  • Data is stored only on the owner's private home server and is never shared, sold or sent to any third party.
  • It never writes, edits or deletes data in Google Health.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms: provided as-is for personal use by the owner only.
